# Complete syllabus and prerequisite map

Five sequential independent-study courses. Allow approximately 5–8 hours per unit, with additional listening and live interaction as needed. Levels are design targets, not guaranteed outcomes or official certification. Qualified educator review is pending.

The prerequisite of each course’s first unit is the preceding course’s final unit. These recommendations do not lock experienced learners out of lessons.

## Spanish I — Foundations (A1-oriented)

### 1. Sounds, spelling & survival phrases

Prerequisite: No prior Spanish. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u01.json).

- Pronounce the five Spanish vowels consistently in familiar words.
- Locate the stressed syllable in familiar words using spelling and accent marks.
- Spell your name and ask a partner to repeat or slow down.
- Open and close a brief polite exchange using four survival phrases.

### 2. Greetings, introductions & ser

Prerequisite: c1-u01. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u02.json).

- Introduce yourself and one other person with ser.
- Choose a subject pronoun for familiar, polite, and plural address.
- Ask and answer about origin and occupation.

### 3. Nouns, articles & agreement

Prerequisite: c1-u02. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u03.json).

- Choose definite and indefinite articles for familiar nouns.
- Form common noun and adjective plurals.
- Describe two objects with agreeing adjectives.

### 4. Numbers, dates & time

Prerequisite: c1-u03. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u04.json).

- Exchange a date and time using familiar numbers.
- Give an age with tener as a learned phrase.
- Clarify ambiguous numeric information.

### 5. The present tense & daily life

Prerequisite: c1-u04. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u05.json).

- Identify an infinitive and a conjugated verb.
- Conjugate regular ar, er, and ir verbs in the present.
- Describe a typical day with time expressions.

### 6. Questions, negation & midterm

Prerequisite: c1-u05. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u06.json).

- Ask information and yes/no questions.
- Use no, nunca, and nadie in basic negatives.
- Combine Units 1–5 in an information-gap exchange.

### 7. Places, estar & hay

Prerequisite: c1-u06. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u07.json).

- Locate people and objects with estar.
- Introduce existence with hay.
- Explain basic ser/estar contrasts without a permanent/temporary shortcut.

### 8. Family, descriptions & tener

Prerequisite: c1-u07. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u08.json).

- Use tener for possessions, age, and common physical needs.
- Describe a family or chosen community without assuming one family structure.
- Use possessive adjectives with the possessed noun.

### 9. Interests, gustar & invitations

Prerequisite: c1-u08. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u09.json).

- Choose gusta or gustan according to what is liked.
- Identify the person experiencing a preference.
- Invite someone and respond with a reason.

### 10. Routines & irregular verbs

Prerequisite: c1-u09. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u10.json).

- Use common irregular present forms in a daily narrative.
- Match reflexive pronouns to the subject.
- Distinguish habitual present statements from a basic ongoing-action form.

### 11. Food, shopping & future plans

Prerequisite: c1-u10. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u11.json).

- Make a practical request and confirm a price.
- Use ir a plus infinitive for a plan.
- Ask about an ingredient or available alternative.

### 12. Your first conversations

Prerequisite: c1-u11. [Full original unit](../content/units/c1-u12.json).

- Sustain a short introduction with clarification and follow-up.
- Write an organized personal profile using familiar language.
- Identify strengths and priorities from multiple forms of evidence.

Assessments: Entry check (6 controlled questions plus a performance task), Cumulative midterm (18 controlled questions plus a performance task), Cumulative final (24 controlled questions plus a performance task).

## Spanish II — Everyday communication (A2-oriented)

### 1. Present-tense changes

Prerequisite: c1-u12. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u01.json).

- Use frequent stem-changing verbs in a routine.
- Distinguish yo-only irregularity from a stem change.
- Ask a follow-up about a person’s schedule.

### 2. Reflexive routines

Prerequisite: c2-u01. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u02.json).

- Use reflexive pronouns with routines and infinitives.
- Distinguish an action on oneself from an action on another person.
- Negotiate a practical shared routine.

### 3. Direct-object pronouns

Prerequisite: c2-u02. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u03.json).

- Identify direct objects in familiar sentences.
- Choose and place lo, la, los and las.
- Clarify a pronoun’s referent in a transaction.

### 4. Indirect & combined pronouns

Prerequisite: c2-u03. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u04.json).

- Distinguish recipient from direct object.
- Combine indirect and direct pronouns in the correct order.
- Confirm who receives an item or message.

### 5. Comparisons & quantities

Prerequisite: c2-u04. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u05.json).

- Compare qualities and quantities accurately.
- Use irregular comparative forms.
- Recommend an option using two relevant criteria.

### 6. Regular preterite & midterm

Prerequisite: c2-u05. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u06.json).

- Form regular preterite verbs with accurate accents.
- Present a bounded past event in sequence.
- Complete a cumulative information exchange without notes.

### 7. Irregular preterite

Prerequisite: c2-u06. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u07.json).

- Use frequent irregular preterite stems.
- Distinguish ir and ser through context.
- Clarify a sequence when an event is unexpected.

### 8. Imperfect & past habits

Prerequisite: c2-u07. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u08.json).

- Form the imperfect, including ser, ir and ver.
- Describe past routines and circumstances.
- Avoid turning an English translation cue into a tense rule.

### 9. Telling connected stories

Prerequisite: c2-u08. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u09.json).

- Choose past-tense viewpoint according to narrative purpose.
- Connect background and a bounded event.
- Retell an event with clarification and an ending.

### 10. Commands & everyday advice

Prerequisite: c2-u09. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u10.json).

- Form common affirmative and negative commands.
- Place object pronouns in instructions.
- Adapt advice to audience and relationship.

### 11. Por, para & present perfect

Prerequisite: c2-u10. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u11.json).

- Distinguish common por and para meanings in context.
- Form the present perfect with haber and a participle.
- Recognize regional variation in recent-past framing.

### 12. Travel & storytelling project

Prerequisite: c2-u11. [Full original unit](../content/units/c2-u12.json).

- Integrate present, past, pronouns and practical requests.
- Tell a coherent travel or local-exploration story.
- Use evidence to choose the next review priorities.

Assessments: Entry check (12 controlled questions plus a performance task), Cumulative midterm (18 controlled questions plus a performance task), Cumulative final (24 controlled questions plus a performance task).

## Spanish III — Independent communication (B1-oriented)

### 1. Connected past narration

Prerequisite: c2-u12. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u01.json).

- Maintain a coherent past timeline.
- Distinguish the narrator’s knowledge from inference.
- Respond to clarification while retelling an event.

### 2. Perfect tenses & sequence

Prerequisite: c3-u01. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u02.json).

- Form present and past perfect tenses.
- Locate an earlier event relative to a past reference point.
- Distinguish participles with haber from agreeing adjectives.

### 3. Future forms & predictions

Prerequisite: c3-u02. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u03.json).

- Form regular and frequent irregular futures.
- Choose between present, ir a and future according to context.
- Signal prediction rather than certainty.

### 4. Conditional & possibilities

Prerequisite: c3-u03. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u04.json).

- Form conditional verbs with regular and irregular stems.
- Make courteous requests and recommendations.
- Distinguish a tentative proposal from a commitment.

### 5. Relative clauses

Prerequisite: c3-u04. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u05.json).

- Build relative clauses with que, quien and donde.
- Distinguish identifying from supplementary information.
- Repair an ambiguous reference.

### 6. Interview & narrative midterm

Prerequisite: c3-u05. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u06.json).

- Ask open questions and relevant follow-ups.
- Transform interview notes into a coherent account.
- Attribute information and mark missing evidence.

### 7. Present subjunctive

Prerequisite: c3-u06. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u07.json).

- Form common present subjunctives.
- Contrast asserted information with wishes or evaluation.
- Choose an infinitive when the subject remains the same.

### 8. Wishes, emotion & influence

Prerequisite: c3-u07. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u08.json).

- Express wishes, emotion and recommendations with appropriate clauses.
- Distinguish infinitive and changed-subject structures.
- Respond respectfully when advice is declined.

### 9. Doubt, purpose & clauses

Prerequisite: c3-u08. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u09.json).

- Use subjunctive in selected doubt and purpose clauses.
- Contrast known and sought antecedents.
- Distinguish habitual from anticipated temporal events.

### 10. Impersonal & passive se

Prerequisite: c3-u09. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u10.json).

- Distinguish impersonal from passive se constructions.
- Use agreement in common passive-se sentences.
- Explain an institutional process clearly.

### 11. Opinions & conversation repair

Prerequisite: c3-u10. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u11.json).

- State an opinion with a reason and example.
- Acknowledge a different position without falsely agreeing.
- Use repair strategies during spontaneous discussion.

### 12. Problem-solving presentation

Prerequisite: c3-u11. [Full original unit](../content/units/c3-u12.json).

- Define a problem and compare feasible responses.
- Present a supported recommendation with uncertainty.
- Respond to unprepared questions and revise the plan.

Assessments: Entry check (12 controlled questions plus a performance task), Cumulative midterm (18 controlled questions plus a performance task), Cumulative final (24 controlled questions plus a performance task).

## Spanish IV — Confident discussion (B2-oriented)

### 1. Tense, aspect & mood

Prerequisite: c3-u12. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u01.json).

- Explain how tense, aspect and mood change a message.
- Distinguish evidence from an attitude toward it.
- Revise a narrative for a different communicative purpose.

### 2. Imperfect subjunctive

Prerequisite: c4-u01. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u02.json).

- Form imperfect subjunctives in ra and se.
- Use past reporting with dependent wishes and recommendations.
- Recognize polite imperfect-subjunctive chunks without overgeneralizing.

### 3. Real & hypothetical si clauses

Prerequisite: c4-u02. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u03.json).

- Build open and hypothetical si clauses.
- Avoid future or conditional in the standard open si clause.
- Negotiate a conditional agreement with clear commitments.

### 4. Perfect subjunctive & counterfactuals

Prerequisite: c4-u03. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u04.json).

- Form present and past perfect subjunctives.
- Express a completed event within an evaluative clause.
- Discuss counterfactual past outcomes without confusing them with facts.

### 5. Reported speech

Prerequisite: c4-u04. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u05.json).

- Transform direct speech while preserving reference and stance.
- Adjust time/place expressions when the context changes.
- Avoid mechanical tense changes that alter current facts.

### 6. Multiple-source midterm

Prerequisite: c4-u05. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u06.json).

- Compare claims, methods and scopes across sources.
- Explain agreement and disagreement without forcing a consensus.
- Produce an attributed synthesis and discuss its limits.

### 7. Connectors & argument

Prerequisite: c4-u06. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u07.json).

- Organize claim, support, concession and conclusion.
- Choose connectors by logical relationship.
- Avoid presenting correlation as established causation.

### 8. Register & professional writing

Prerequisite: c4-u07. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u08.json).

- Adapt register to recipient, purpose and medium.
- Write a concise professional request with a clear action.
- Revise wording that obscures responsibility or tone.

### 9. News & differing viewpoints

Prerequisite: c4-u08. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u09.json).

- Identify framing and attribution in news or interviews.
- Separate a source’s assertion from the presenter’s wording.
- Summarize opposing positions without caricature.

### 10. Debate & negotiation

Prerequisite: c4-u09. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u10.json).

- Rebut an argument without attacking the speaker.
- Make conditional concessions and clarify agreements.
- Ask and answer spontaneous follow-up questions.

### 11. Culture & regional variation

Prerequisite: c4-u10. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u11.json).

- Distinguish regional, social and situational variation.
- Recognize voseo and multiple address systems.
- Revise literal translations that distort meaning or tone.

### 12. Persuasive presentation

Prerequisite: c4-u11. [Full original unit](../content/units/c4-u12.json).

- Deliver an organized persuasive presentation.
- Use sources proportionately and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respond to rebuttal and revise a recommendation.

Assessments: Entry check (12 controlled questions plus a performance task), Cumulative midterm (18 controlled questions plus a performance task), Cumulative final (24 controlled questions plus a performance task).

## Advanced Spanish — Academic & professional practice (B2–C1 stretch)

### 1. Diagnostic & improvement plan

Prerequisite: c4-u12. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u01.json).

- Collect comparable samples across language skills.
- Distinguish recurring errors from isolated slips.
- Set a measurable improvement goal without claiming a certified level.

### 2. Extended listening & notes

Prerequisite: c5-u01. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u02.json).

- Identify an extended speaker’s organization and stance.
- Take selective notes without attempting a transcript.
- Summarize with accurate attribution and uncertainty.

### 3. Complex reading & arguments

Prerequisite: c5-u02. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u03.json).

- Identify thesis, evidence, assumptions and counterarguments.
- Distinguish a descriptive claim from a recommendation.
- Evaluate whether a conclusion exceeds its support.

### 4. Multiple-source synthesis

Prerequisite: c5-u03. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u04.json).

- Organize multiple sources around a research question.
- Attribute facts, interpretations and uncertainty accurately.
- Write a synthesis that preserves consequential differences.

### 5. Academic & professional writing

Prerequisite: c5-u04. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u05.json).

- Plan academic or professional writing around audience and purpose.
- Use paragraph structure and appropriate qualification.
- Revise for coherence, accuracy and traceable sources.

### 6. Research briefing midterm

Prerequisite: c5-u05. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u06.json).

- Deliver a concise research briefing with source distinctions.
- Answer questions without inventing unsupported details.
- Document how discussion changes the recommendation.

### 7. Presentations & questions

Prerequisite: c5-u06. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u07.json).

- Design an oral presentation for a specific audience.
- Use signposting without reading a complete script.
- Respond directly to unexpected questions and acknowledge limits.

### 8. Nuance, idioms & pragmatics

Prerequisite: c5-u07. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u08.json).

- Distinguish literal meaning from pragmatic effect.
- Adjust idiomatic language and register to the audience.
- Interpret indirectness without assuming a single cultural rule.

### 9. Workplace collaboration

Prerequisite: c5-u08. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u09.json).

- Conduct a professional interview with relevant follow-ups.
- Negotiate responsibilities, deadlines and fallback plans.
- Record agreements without turning tentative language into promises.

### 10. Literature, film & humor

Prerequisite: c5-u09. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u10.json).

- Distinguish narrator, character and author.
- Support an interpretation with specific textual or audiovisual evidence.
- Explain ambiguity, irony or humor without claiming one universal reading.

### 11. Conversation & error repair

Prerequisite: c5-u10. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u11.json).

- Sustain an extended exchange across topic shifts.
- Identify recurring errors by communicative effect.
- Practice repair without sacrificing participation.

### 12. Portfolio & capstone

Prerequisite: c5-u11. [Full original unit](../content/units/c5-u12.json).

- Select evidence that supports specific development claims.
- Deliver a capstone and respond spontaneously.
- Create a sustainable continuation plan with honest limitations.

Assessments: Entry check (12 controlled questions plus a performance task), Cumulative midterm (18 controlled questions plus a performance task), Cumulative final (24 controlled questions plus a performance task).
